Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Joe&dave Operations Intelligence (“Joe&dave”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our website, progressive web app, mobile app wrapper and business operations services.

Important: Joe&dave is a business recordkeeping service. Do not upload information you do not have authority to process. You remain responsible for the accuracy and lawful collection of customer, employee, supplier and payroll information entered into your workspace.

1. Information we process

  • Account information: email address, password hash, session identifiers and account/workspace membership details.
  • Business information: business name, contact details, currency, branding and configuration.
  • Operational records: customer and supplier details, invoices, receipts, expenses, inventory, purchase orders, projects, support tickets and other records you create.
  • Workforce information: employee profiles, attendance, leave, claims and payroll records where your organisation chooses to use those modules.
  • Technical and security information: request timestamps, authentication failure counters, audit events, browser/device storage and service diagnostics needed to operate and secure the service.
  • Local device data: some interface preferences and invoice drafts may be stored in your browser. Local drafts are not sent to our servers until you submit the related transaction.

2. Why we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, provide business workspaces, process transactions you instruct us to save, generate invoices and reports, enforce role-based access, maintain audit trails, troubleshoot faults, prevent abuse, respond to support requests and meet legal obligations.

3. Legal basis and responsibilities

Where applicable, processing is based on performing the service you request, our legitimate interests in security and service operation, your organisation’s instructions, consent where required, and compliance with legal obligations. If you enter personal data about customers, employees or suppliers, your organisation is generally responsible for determining the lawful basis, giving required notices and handling those individuals’ rights. We process that information to provide the service.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell personal information. The service uses Cloudflare Pages, Workers and D1 for website delivery, API execution and database storage. Information may be processed by those infrastructure providers to host and secure the service. We may disclose information where required by law, to protect users and the service, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards. We do not currently represent that a payment processor or marketing analytics provider is connected to the production application.

5. Storage, security and international transfers

Passwords are stored as salted PBKDF2 hashes, not plaintext. Session tokens are stored as hashes on the API side. Workspace access is enforced by server-side membership and role checks. We use encrypted HTTPS connections for the hosted service and apply technical controls intended to prevent unauthorised access. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Cloud hosting may process data in regions outside Nigeria; applicable transfer safeguards and local law requirements should be assessed by your organisation.

6. Retention and backups

We retain account and business records while needed to provide the service, maintain auditability, resolve disputes, and comply with accounting, tax, employment and other legal requirements. Deleting a login does not automatically mean that underlying invoices, receipts, payroll records or audit evidence can lawfully be erased. Backup copies may remain for a limited period and are protected as operational recovery copies; they are not used as a substitute for normal access controls.

7. Your choices and rights

You can review and correct many business records in your workspace. You may request access, correction, restriction or deletion of personal information, subject to identity verification, the rights of other people, legal retention duties and legitimate business-record requirements. An account owner can submit an account-deletion request from Workspace settings. Requests are reviewed before processing so that business ownership, shared workspaces and legally required records are not destroyed accidentally. You can cancel a pending request in the workspace before it is processed.

8. Children

Joe&dave is intended for business use and is not designed for children. Do not create an account for a child or enter children’s personal information unless your organisation has a lawful basis and appropriate safeguards.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service, law or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when a material update is published. Continued use after an update is subject to the revised policy to the extent permitted by law.

10. Contact and complaints

For privacy questions or requests, sign in and submit a message through the in-app Helpdesk/support area, selecting “Privacy request” in the subject. Include the account email and describe the right you wish to exercise; do not send passwords or full payment-card details. You may also contact the relevant data protection regulator, including the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, where applicable.

Before broad commercial launch, the service operator should publish its registered legal identity, registered address and dedicated privacy contact. This policy describes the current technical service and is not legal advice.
